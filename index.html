How we work together

A clear process from our first conversation to going live.

Each stage produces a clear decision or deliverable, so scope, cost, progress, and launch responsibilities stay visible throughout the project.

01

Understand the need

We discuss your business, the problem to solve, target users, priorities, and expected outcome.

02

Confirm the scope

I organize the requirements with you and confirm what the first release will include and exclude.

03

Document and quote

You receive a software requirements confirmation document, proposed delivery plan, and quotation.

04

Confirm the project

Once the scope, quotation, timeline, and working terms are accepted, we officially begin.

05

Design the solution

I plan the user experience, interface, technical architecture, data, and key system flows.

06

Build the business logic

The product is developed around the confirmed workflows, rules, integrations, and real use cases.

07

Deploy for testing

A testing environment is deployed so you can review the complete product and verify each requirement.

08

Prepare production

After testing is accepted, we arrange the production server and domain under your ownership.

09

Launch

I deploy the production system, connect the domain, run final checks, and bring your product online.
